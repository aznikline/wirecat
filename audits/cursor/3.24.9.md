_rule: inconsistent-sink-validation · confidence: observed_

When a sibling path enforces a policy the unguarded path bypasses it entirely — a classic "two registration paths, one guarded" pattern (verified live on Tencent WorkBuddy: direct-IPC had scheme allowlist + executable blocklist, the RPC path had neither).
